# Story Architecture Expansion Design

**Date:** 2026-09-18  
**Task class:** STRUCTURAL_CHANGE plus planning/canon-support setup  
**Status:** Approved by the author's ten-point scope

## Goal

Expand the pre-production story workspace with durable writing rules, comedy guidance, planned mystery and foreshadowing tracking, world and character history planning, power-system authority, non-System progression planning, and a provisional 300-chapter roadmap split across ten volumes.

## Authority boundary

The repository workflow remains controlled by AGENTS.md and 00_PROJECT/. The master story plan remains the highest story-direction source currently available. These additions may organize, clarify, and track approved master-plan material, but they may not silently invent canon.

A planned ledger entry is not canonized prose. Foreshadowing uses PLANNED until approved prose contains the seed, then PLANTED, REINFORCED, PAYOFF_DUE, or PAID_OFF as evidence permits. Mysteries remain OPEN or PARTIALLY_REVEALED until prose changes their status.

The 300-chapter roadmap is an author-approved planning horizon. It is not a locked final chapter count, and Chapter 300 is a checkpoint with continuation hooks for Chapter 301 and beyond.

## Design units

### Writing authority

- Expand 06_REFERENCES/WRITING_STYLE_GUIDE.md with natural-author prose, close-POV, dialogue, exposition, rhythm, emotional continuity, and revision rules.
- Add 06_REFERENCES/WRITING_RULES.md as the practical drafting contract, including the 1,000-word chapter target and fourth-wall progression guard.
- Add comedy rules that preserve consequences, avoid constant quips, and make humor arise from character, timing, coping, and social friction.

### History and character authority

- Add 03_WORLD/HISTORY_PLAN.md for world, System, divine, Fate, Great Design, Demon King cycles, mortal civilization, and reincarnation history.
- Add 02_CHARACTERS/CHARACTER_HISTORY_INDEX.md for the protagonist, summoning authority, future companions, guild figures, demon leadership, and divine figures. Unknown names and biography facts remain unknown.
- Add 02_CHARACTERS/CHARACTER_HISTORY_TEMPLATE.md for individual history records, knowledge boundaries, secrets, wounds, relationships, and reveal permissions.

### Power and progression authority

- Add 04_POWER_SYSTEMS/POWER_SYSTEM_BIBLE.md for System terminology, regeneration, magic, Fate, Great Design, counters, costs, and limits.
- Add 05_EVOLUTION/PROGRESSION_ROADMAP.md for non-System improvement, identity progression, fourth-wall stages, and future boundaries. No levels, hidden class, or secret System evolution may be introduced.

### 300-chapter roadmap

Create 08_PLANNING/300_CHAPTER_ROADMAP.md with:

- ten provisional volumes;
- thirty chapter slots per volume;
- Volumes 1-2 mapped to Arc 1;
- Volumes 3-4 mapped to Arc 2;
- Volumes 5-6 mapped to Arc 3;
- Volumes 7-8 mapped to Arc 4;
- Volumes 9-10 mapped to Arc 5;
- six five-chapter movement blocks per volume;
- volume purpose, identity movement, power boundary, memory/foreshadowing focus, and continuation hooks;
- Chapter 300 as a major checkpoint rather than final resolution;
- unresolved mysteries, unexplored regions, future factions, and Chapter 301+ hooks.

The roadmap may contain provisional working directions, but it must label them as provisional and never treat future power, knowledge, or reveal as current entry state.

## Validation

- Existing style guide still preserves the master-plan tone and slow fourth-wall progression.
- Writing rules explicitly enforce natural prose and the 1,000-word minimum.
- Every new MY/FS entry is marked planned/open rather than planted/resolved.
- History and character files preserve unknown facts.
- Power-system rules preserve UNDEFINED, no normal System progression, regeneration counters, and Fate non-immunity.
- Roadmap contains 10 volumes and 30 chapter slots per volume, with five arcs mapped two volumes each.
- Chapter 300 has an open continuation contract.
- No manuscript prose, synopsis, or export is created.
- No Docker files are added; Docker is unrelated to this story-documentation scope.
