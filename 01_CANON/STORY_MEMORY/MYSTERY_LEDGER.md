# Mystery Ledger

These records organize unresolved questions already listed in the master story plan. They are planning memory, not new canon. Every entry is `OPEN` until approved prose changes its status through the required context and QA workflow.

## Status rules

- `OPEN`: the question is active and no authorized answer has been revealed;
- `PARTIALLY_REVEALED`: approved prose has narrowed the answer without resolving it;
- `RESOLVED`: approved prose and exit-state QA record the answer and its consequences;
- exact chapter references are required for every status change;
- author-only truth is not character knowledge;
- no mystery may be resolved only to complete a roadmap slot.
