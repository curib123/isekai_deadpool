# Foreshadowing Ledger

These are planned continuity seeds derived from the master plan. `PLANNED` means the intended seed exists in planning only. It does not mean approved prose has planted it. Change a status only after exact prose references, reader knowledge, character knowledge, and consequences are reconciled.

## Status rules

- `PLANNED`: approved planning intention; no prose seed exists yet;
- `PLANTED`: approved prose contains the seed;
- `REINFORCED`: approved prose adds a compatible reminder or clue;
- `PAYOFF_DUE`: setup is sufficient for an approved payoff decision;
- `PAID_OFF`: payoff and immediate consequences are canonized;
- exact chapter references are required for every movement;
- a planned seed cannot create current character knowledge or a future power.

## FS-001 - Repeated UNDEFINED results

- seed: different System checks repeatedly return `UNDEFINED` for the protagonist;
- source authority: master story plan, Arc 1;
- status: `PLANNED`;
- first-use boundary: Arc 1 guild examination planning;
- later movement: repetition becomes institutional evidence and supports the Glitch nickname;
- payoff condition: a later authority recognizes the pattern without turning it into normal classification;
- consequence requirement: suspicion, policy, reputation, or investigation must change;
- exact chapter references: none yet.
