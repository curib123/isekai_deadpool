# Story Quality Checklist

Before accepting any future chapter:
1. Does it follow the World Bible?
2. Does it avoid contradicting established lore?
3. Does something meaningful happen?
4. Is it connected to prior or future material?
5. Does at least one character develop, reveal, decide, or react meaningfully?
6. Is there tension, mystery, emotion, humor, discovery, conflict, or atmosphere?
7. Does dialogue avoid repetitive voices?
8. Is progression earned?
9. Are Skills/Evolution consistent?
10. Are major antagonists understandable rather than evil-by-default?
11. Are relevant previous characters/events remembered?
12. Is foreshadowing useful rather than random?
13. Does the ending create interest without repeating the same cliffhanger pattern?
