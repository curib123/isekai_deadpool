# Human-Prose Gate

> **Authority:** QA checklist only. `06_REFERENCES/WRITING_STYLE_GUIDE.md` remains the sole active prose authority. This file does not define canon and cannot override canon, continuity, character authority, progression/Evolution authority, or locked manuscript facts.

Use this gate after drafting or presentation-only revision and before chapter/arc certification. Context decides whether a prose construction is natural and effective; metrics and pattern counts are diagnostic signals, not automatic pass/fail rules.

## Required Result

```text
Human-prose paragraph flow: PASS
Sentence rhythm: PASS
Emotional/physical continuity: PASS
Character-specific dialogue: PASS
Direct-thought restraint: PASS
Natural wording: PASS
Cinematic restraint: PASS
AI-pattern/redundancy review: PASS
```

## Review Meaning

- **Human-prose paragraph flow:** connected narration is the default; paragraph breaks mark meaningful shifts; earned isolated beats may remain isolated.
- **Sentence rhythm:** short, medium, and occasional longer sentences vary naturally rather than following a repeated template.
- **Emotional/physical continuity:** established emotion is experienced through the character's body, attention, behavior, speech, and current sensory limits without inventing a new canonical emotional event.
- **Character-specific dialogue:** dialogue remains conversational, voice-specific, knowledge-safe, and faithful to what the locked scene actually communicates.
- **Direct-thought restraint:** exact first-person italic thoughts are selective and do not merely restate narration.
- **Natural wording:** ordinary precise language is preferred over inflated or unnecessarily formal wording.
- **Cinematic restraint:** fragments, isolation, and heightened presentation are reserved for moments that genuinely earn them.
- **AI-pattern/redundancy review:** repeated stock frames, micro-paragraph defaults, mirrored constructions, artificial punchlines, duplicated explanation, and similar mechanical patterns were reviewed in context.

## Locked Manuscript Addendum

For any locked-manuscript revision, the chapter/arc ledger must additionally record:

```text
Canon changed: NO
```

A prose improvement that would change events, causes, chronology, outcomes, knowledge, relationships, injuries, costs, progression, Evolution timing, mysteries, foreshadowing, System mechanics, or protected fixed-history facts must be rejected.
