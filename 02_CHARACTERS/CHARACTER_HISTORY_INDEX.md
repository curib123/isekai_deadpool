## Character-history movement rules

- A history reveal must change present behavior, trust, fear, allegiance, or decision-making.
- A recovered memory is not automatically a complete or reliable biography.
- Character knowledge must be separated from reader knowledge and author-only planning truth.
- No companion exists only to deliver exposition; each history must create present pressure or relationship consequence.
- Unknown names, family histories, factions, and past events remain unknown until an authority establishes them.
- A character's past cannot silently rewrite a locked identity rule, System rule, or prior exit state.
