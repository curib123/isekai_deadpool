# Story Structure

This file defines the repository layout for the standalone serial project. It is structural authority, not a replacement for the master plan.

## Current structure

```text
00_PROJECT/          workflow and authority
01_CANON/            approved world and story truth
02_CHARACTERS/       character authority
03_WORLD/            locations, factions, cultures, and history
04_POWER_SYSTEMS/    System, magic, Fate, regeneration, and related rules
05_EVOLUTION/        earned non-System growth and progression records
06_REFERENCES/       prose and publication guidance
07_TEMPLATES/        context, book, arc, chapter, and entity templates
08_PLANNING/         master plan and approved arc plans
09_FUTURE_STORY/     numbered manuscript when drafting begins
10_QA/               checklists, ledgers, and certification evidence
11_STORY_STUDIO/     optional story tooling and context infrastructure
WEBNOVEL_EXPORT/     generated publication exports
scripts/             project-specific QA and build helpers
docs/                design and implementation documentation
```

## Status boundaries

The five arcs currently described in the master plan are roadmap material. They are not chapter prose, final canon, or publication certification. Do not invent a chapter count or book boundary until the author approves that structure.
