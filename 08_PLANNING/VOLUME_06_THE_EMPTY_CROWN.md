# Volume 06 - Red Jackal Tells You: The Empty Crown

## Destination-arc authority

- volume number: 06;
- chapter range: 151-180;
- status: PROVISIONAL DESTINATION-ARC AUTHORITY;
- destination: the ancient kingdom of Veyr, an abandoned royal center of preserved streets, dead ceremonies, empty palaces, and a population divided by old succession law;
- local problem: the empty crown continues to command a dead army and prevents the living kingdom from choosing a future;
- primary antagonist: `The Crownless Regent` - a provisional caretaker who believes repeating the old order is kinder than allowing history to change;
- local resolution: the crown cycle is broken, transferred, or contained and the living people receive an actual political choice;
- overarching clue: royal records describe reincarnation and identity as choices made by institutions, not only by souls;
- departure: Red Jackal follows a buried route toward the Underdeep;
- power boundary: his unlimited imagination cannot be reduced to a hidden class or royal artifact;
- prose boundary: recovered memories remain partial and do not resolve his original identity.

## Pacing and comedy

Use palace infiltration, undead processions, collapsing ceremonial halls, duels, rebellions, and battles over who gets to define history. Comedy comes from etiquette, royal furniture, ceremonial nonsense, bad ghost manners, and Red Jackal pretending to understand court politics. Death, grief, and the burden of inherited history remain serious.
