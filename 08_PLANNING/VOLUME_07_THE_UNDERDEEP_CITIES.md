# Volume 07 - Red Jackal Tells You: The Underdeep Cities

## Destination-arc authority

- volume number: 07;
- chapter range: 181-210;
- status: PROVISIONAL DESTINATION-ARC AUTHORITY;
- destination: the Underdeep Cities, an underground civilization of layered caverns, artificial suns, buried rivers, old registries, and cultures that never see the surface;
- local problem: the failing light and food routes turn an old political division into a survival crisis;
- primary antagonist: `The Buried Assembly` - a provisional ruling body that would sacrifice one city to preserve the system supporting the others;
- local resolution: the resource crisis and political choice receive a local resolution with a visible cost;
- overarching clue: the oldest registry beneath the world contains a record showing that some beings were never designed to fit the System;
- departure: the cast rises toward a divine region through a route opened by the undercity conflict;
- power boundary: Red Jackal can create any solution he imagines, but other people still decide what they can accept and live with;
- prose boundary: the Underdeep's species, languages, and history remain provisional until chapter authority.

## Pacing and comedy

Use cave pursuits, bridge fights, vertical chases, light failures, monster tunnels, and faction clashes. Comedy comes from cramped living, elevator-like magic, food arguments, class misunderstandings, and Red Jackal trying to be dramatic in places with very low ceilings. Starvation, sacrifice, and political damage remain real.
