## Volume creation gate

Before creating a numbered chapter:

- a parent book/volume authority exists;
- the volume title and chapter range are author-approved;
- the target arc plan exists beneath this planning layer;
- the inherited entry state is recorded;
- unknowns and contradictions affecting the chapter are resolved or isolated;
- the chapter plan records a minimum prose target of 1,000 words.

For Volume 01, the parent-authority gate is met for structural recheck, synopsis, prologue, and Chapter 001 work through their task-specific context and entry-state gates. Later volumes have destination authorities but remain `PROVISIONAL` for prose authorization until their entry states and local antagonists are approved.
