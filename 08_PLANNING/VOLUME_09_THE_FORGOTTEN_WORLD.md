# Volume 09 - Red Jackal Tells You: The Forgotten World

## Destination-arc authority

- volume number: 09;
- chapter range: 241-270;
- status: PROVISIONAL DESTINATION-ARC AUTHORITY;
- destination: the Forgotten World, an isolated territory removed from ordinary maps where history, language, and roads disappear when no one remembers them;
- local problem: the inhabitants are being erased from records and from one another's memories while the territory collapses;
- primary antagonist: `The Last Cartographer` - a provisional keeper who erases dangerous places to protect the rest of the world;
- local resolution: the territory's erasure is stopped or given a chosen ending by the people who live there;
- overarching clue: the forgotten maps show a route leading directly toward the Great Design's outer boundary;
- departure: the cast enters the final destination arc with an unresolved choice about what must be remembered;
- power boundary: Red Jackal's imagination remains unlimited, but identity, memory, and consent make every choice meaningful;
- prose boundary: the forgotten world's true age and original connection to reincarnation remain open.

## Pacing and comedy

Use memory chases, collapsing roads, forgotten monsters, map duels, rescue missions, and battles against disappearing landmarks. Comedy comes from lost names, bad directions, awkward introductions, and Red Jackal complaining about maps that refuse to behave. Erasure, grief, and lost relationships remain serious.
