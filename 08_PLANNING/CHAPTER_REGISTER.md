## Future slot format

When the author approves a chapter breakdown, each slot must record:

- chapter number and approved title;
- parent volume and arc;
- chapter purpose;
- inherited entry state;
- permitted event, progression, mystery, and foreshadowing movement;
- protected facts;
- planned exit-state delta;
- minimum prose target: 1,000 words;
- actual prose word count after drafting;
- QA and certification status.

No future slot may silently establish a character, location, power, relationship, reveal, travel event, or timeline position absent from its parent authority. Use `UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED` for missing facts and `CONTRADICTION - AUTHOR DECISION REQUIRED` for equal-authority conflicts.
