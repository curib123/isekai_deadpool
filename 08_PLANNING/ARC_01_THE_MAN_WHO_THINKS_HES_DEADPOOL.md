2. His Deadpool memories remain unusually clear.
3. His regeneration convinces him that he must actually be Deadpool.
4. A goddess rejects that identity; he refuses to accept the explanation.
5. He reaches the mortal world and encounters an adventurer guild.
6. Guild examinations repeatedly return UNDEFINED through different examiners or equipment.
7. He adopts Deadpool as his name despite the unresolved identity question.
8. He receives the lowest practical adventurer classification because the guild cannot classify him normally.
