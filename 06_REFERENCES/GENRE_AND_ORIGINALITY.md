# Genre and Originality Guide

The novel may draw structural inspiration from monster-evolution, reincarnation, progression-fantasy, dark-fantasy, and long-running epic traditions, but Aevareth must keep its own terminology, causal logic, cultures, mythology, species, and conflicts.

Avoid copying protected character identities, signature plots, terminology systems, or scene sequences from another franchise.

Core appeal:
- visible earned transformation;
- survival intelligence;
- branching progression;
- strong aura/payoff scenes;
- interconnected world consequences;
- understandable villains;
- mysteries with planted evidence;
- an increasingly overpowered protagonist challenged by systems, responsibility, information, politics, protection, and specialized counters.
