# Non-System Progression Roadmap

## Authority and status

This roadmap defines earned growth that the System cannot measure. It is provisional planning, not acquired state. A listed stage is not active until approved prose establishes its prerequisites, evidence, cost, and consequence. Any missing prerequisite, milestone, or future acquisition detail remains `UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED`.

## Red Jackal anomaly boundary

Red Jackal's imagination anomaly exists from his first awakening. He can make anything he imagines happen, without cooldown, resource cost, focus requirement, understanding requirement, range restriction, counter, suppression method, exhaustion rule, or known upper limit. This is not non-System progression: it is the foreign-code condition that makes him unreadable to the world's rules.

The roadmap therefore tracks how he chooses to use, understand, hide, misuse, or refuse that unlimited ability. A scene may show him failing to use it because of character choice, ignorance of his own intention, distraction, emotion, mercy, curiosity, humor, or a decision to let events unfold. It may not create a mechanical blocker or retroactive limitation.

## Progression categories

### External capability

- combat experience and timing;
- strategy and tactical thinking;
- weapon proficiency and environmental awareness;
- magic understanding when separately authorized;
- monster and faction knowledge;
- regeneration control and pain tolerance;
- psychological resilience without removing fear or trauma;
- understanding and exploitation of world mechanics.

### Internal identity

1. I am Deadpool.
2. Obviously I'm Deadpool.
3. What if I'm not?
4. I wasn't Deadpool.
5. Then who was I?
6. I remember who I was.
7. But I'm not that person anymore either.
8. I know who I am.

No identity stage is complete because the roadmap names it. The stage must change decisions, relationships, coping, or responsibility.

### Fourth-wall boundary

There is no fourth-wall progression. Red Jackal has literal fourth-wall awareness and narrative-editing authority from the first page, including the origin-world death scene, synopsis, prologue, Chapter 1, and all later chapters. He can rewrite dialogue, scenes, narration, event order, or storyline without an unlock, cost, blocker, or limit.

The roadmap may track how he chooses to use that authority and how other characters react, but it must never treat awareness as locked, acquired, or upgraded. Identity, judgment, relationships, and emotional maturity may progress; the authority does not.

## Progression gate for every milestone

- prerequisite is established by the parent plan;
- the character has a valid way to learn or practice it;
- the scene demonstrates the change rather than announcing it only;
- the cost or counter remains visible;
- the change does not contradict the System restriction;
- the change is recorded in the exit state after prose approval;
- future non-anomaly stages remain locked until their own authority exists; the imagination anomaly is already established and does not unlock through this roadmap.

## Chapter 300 boundary

Chapter 300 may establish that the protagonist has reached a major checkpoint in understanding himself, non-System growth, or Great Design pressure. It must not close every mystery, resolve every faction, reveal every history layer, or define a final ability set. Chapter 301 and later planning remain available.
