# Power-System Bible

## Authority and status

- source authority: `08_PLANNING/MASTER_STORY_PLAN.md`;
- supporting authority: `06_REFERENCES/WRITING_RULES.md`, `05_EVOLUTION/PROGRESSION_ROADMAP.md`, and approved arc plans;
- status: planning authority for future work;
- unestablished spell lists, divine domains, species abilities, formulas, and named powers: `UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED`.

## System terminology

The following terms are locked:

| Term | Meaning | Protagonist boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `UNDEFINED` | A value has no valid definition inside the System's rules | His core status remains this; it is not a hidden level or class |
| `NO RECORD` | The System or a registry finds no matching entry | His soul or identity may produce this during registry searches |
| `FAILED` | An operation could not complete | A failed operation is not proof of a successful secret ability |
| `UNAVAILABLE` | A feature or pathway cannot be assigned | It does not create a substitute progression route |
| `ANOMALY` | The System's highest-level classification of his existence | It is a classification, not an ultimate title or reward |
| The Glitch | An in-world nickname | It is not an official System field |

Do not replace `UNDEFINED` with `UNKNOWN` as the protagonist's primary System status. Unknown means the answer has not been found; undefined means the System never created a valid value for him.

## Protagonist System restrictions

- he does not level;
- he does not receive a normal class, title, stat sheet, hidden class, System evolution, or ultimate System title;
- System operations may fail, return no record, or remain unavailable;
- real growth appears through experience, training, adaptation, knowledge, strategy, combat creativity, regeneration control, and understanding world mechanics;
- System output can describe an attempted operation but cannot grant unearned knowledge to characters;
- no System message may reveal author-only mystery truth before its approved boundary.

## Regeneration

### Established function

Abnormal regeneration prevents ordinary death. It can restore damage that would kill a normal human, but it does not make the protagonist invulnerable or tension-proof.

### Required costs and counters

Future prose may use the following established counter categories when earned by the plan:

- pain and exhaustion;
- fear and psychological trauma;
- restraint, paralysis, and imprisonment;
- sealing and regeneration suppression;
- soul damage and curses;
- dimensional exile;
- memory interference;
- causality manipulation;
- conceptual attacks;
- attacks against existence itself.

Every regeneration victory must leave a cost, changed condition, learned limit, resource pressure, or relationship consequence when the scene's stakes require it.

### Combat use

The protagonist may deliberately use his body as a tactical resource: bait, trap, shield, anchor, or opening. The tactic must remain painful, risky, and situation-dependent. It cannot become a universal answer to every opponent.

## Magic

Magic exists as part of the fantasy setting, but detailed schools, costs, languages, institutions, limits, and counters are `UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED`.

Before adding a named spell or magic rule, future planning must define source, user, cost, preparation, range, failure mode, counter, social consequence, and how the viewpoint character learned it. Magic may not silently become a replacement for the protagonist's missing System progression.

## Fate and the Great Design

Fate is the world's ordinary name for the pressure of predetermined outcomes. Gods call the larger mechanism the Great Design. The protagonist calls it the plot.

Rules:

- he is difficult for Fate to account for because no destiny was created for him;
- he is not immune to causality or Fate;
- other people retain agency even when the Great Design pushes toward an outcome;
- prophecy can be incomplete, contradictory, unstable, or fail around him;
- the Great Design is not a literal human author writing a novel;
- the Great Design's origin, limits, maintenance, and complete architecture remain `UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED`;
- a perceived pattern is not automatically future sight.

## Fourth-wall progression

The permitted progression is:

1. `Imitation` - he copies remembered Deadpool behavior; nothing supernatural is occurring;
2. `Genre Awareness` - he recognizes familiar story patterns;
3. `Coincidence` - predictions become uncomfortable but remain explainable;
4. `Suspicion` - outcomes feel forced or protected;
5. `Narrative Sensitivity` - he senses pressure on events;
6. `Narrative Perception` - he witnesses contradictions, repetitions, or causal abnormalities;
7. `True Anomaly` - his existence directly interacts with the Great Design.

No later stage is current merely because it is listed here. Each stage requires approved scenes, evidence, costs, and consequences.

## Ability acquisition contract

Every future ability, technique, resistance, or permanent adaptation must record:

- source and acquisition condition;
- legal category: Passive, Active, Apex, technique, resistance, trait, or magic;
- practice or mastery evidence;
- resource cost;
- limits and counters;
- compatible existence state;
- immediate consequence;
- parent arc/volume authority;
- exact canonized chapter reference after prose approval.

## Tension safeguards

- no ability solves every problem;
- no counter appears only when convenient without prior possibility or cost;
- a power can win a fight while losing time, trust, information, resources, or safety;
- the protagonist's survival does not guarantee his companions survive;
- future possibilities remain possible, not acquired;
- System failure is not automatic narrative permission to ignore world rules.
