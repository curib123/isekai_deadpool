# World History Plan

## Authority and status

- source authority: `08_PLANNING/MASTER_STORY_PLAN.md`;
- status: planning framework with master-plan facts only;
- detailed dates, names, maps, dynasties, and event chronology: `UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED`;
- canon rule: this plan organizes future history work but does not make an unknown event true.

## History layers

### 1. Pre-existing cosmology

The world contains a System, magic, Fate, divine powers, prophecy, Demon Kings, sacred bloodlines, civilization cycles, and the Great Design. Their original creation order, makers, first conflicts, and exact cosmology are `UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED`.

### 2. System history

The System classifies registered beings and can return `UNDEFINED`, `NO RECORD`, `FAILED`, `UNAVAILABLE`, or `ANOMALY`. The protagonist is not properly registered and does not receive ordinary levels, classes, stat points, or System-granted progression.

The System's creator, first registry, administrative authority, age, maintenance mechanism, and relation to the Great Design are `UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED`. Future history may reveal these only through approved authority and earned clues.

### 3. Divine history

Gods can investigate the protagonist through divination, soul reading, artifacts, and prophecy, but their exact identities, domains, alliances, rivalries, and historical limits are `UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED`.

Divine history must preserve the rule that gods are participants within a larger structure and are not automatically the final enemy.

### 4. Fate and the Great Design

The Great Design is the metaphysical structure that pushes history toward predetermined outcomes. It influences chosen heroes, Demon Kings, prophecies, sacred bloodlines, wars, divine interventions, disasters, kingdom collapses, and historical turning points.

The Great Design was not built with the protagonist in mind. His difficulty with Fate comes from the absence of an assigned destiny, not immunity to causality. Its origin, limits, maintenance, failure modes, and history before his arrival are `UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED`.

### 5. Demon King cycles

The master plan establishes Demon Kings and their forces as part of the world's recurring conflict structure. The number of cycles, prior Demon Kings, causes, kingdoms involved, and relationship to prophecy or the Great Design are `UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED`.

Do not create a historical Demon King list until a future arc or volume authority defines the purpose and evidence for it.

### 6. Mortal civilization

Guilds, kingdoms, adventurers, communities, wars, institutions, and ordinary people experience the consequences of the System, monsters, demons, gods, and Fate. Names, borders, economies, languages, technologies, religions, and historical dates are `UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED`.

Mortal history must show that the world is not a joke. Death, institutional failure, friendship, war, and political consequences remain meaningful even when the protagonist uses comedy as coping.

### 7. Reincarnation event

An ordinary Earth-born human fan awakens in a fantasy world with severe memory loss, clear Deadpool memories, an unreadable or unregistered soul, abnormal regeneration, and a foreign-code imagination anomaly. The exact transition, summoning authority, selection mechanism, missing-memory cause, reason for the memory imbalance, and source of the virus-like intrusion are `UNKNOWN / NOT ESTABLISHED`.

The protagonist is never secretly Wade Wilson. The reincarnation event must preserve this locked identity rule.

## Knowledge layers

Every future history record must separate:

- reader knowledge: what approved prose has shown;
- character knowledge: what a character has validly learned;
- faction knowledge: what an institution or group can reasonably know;
- author-only planning truth: a planned possibility not yet available to prose or characters.

Author-only history must not leak into narration, dialogue, System output, prophecy, or character decisions before its approved reveal boundary.

## History research and creation gates

Before adding a historical event:

- identify its parent authority;
- define who knows it and how they know it;
- link affected `MY-####` and `FS-####` records when relevant;
- define its present-day consequence;
- define what remains unknown;
- record whether it is planned, canonized in prose, or merely a future possibility.

## Open history questions

- why regeneration was assigned to him;
- who or what initiated the reincarnation;

- which regions, cultures, and factions remain outside the current roadmap;
